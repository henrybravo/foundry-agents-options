# Hike proposal HP-2026-07 - Ridge Lake traverse

Fictional demo document. Alpenglow Trail Club and the places named here are fictitious.

## Summary

Alpenglow Trail Club proposes a one-day group traverse of the Ridge Lake
circuit on Saturday 17 October 2026, open to all members.

## Proposed plan

1. **Schedule.** The group meets at the trailhead at 11:00. The circuit is
   22 km at an average pace of 3 km/h including breaks. Sunset on the day
   is 18:40.
2. **Route.** The route crosses the Ridge Lake nature reserve. The club has
   not yet applied for a reserve permit and the group may leave the marked
   path to reach the lake shore for photos. There is no mobile signal on
   the ridge section, and no emergency exit point is named.
3. **Group.** Up to 30 members may join. Two trained leaders will lead the
   hike. The route passes through an alpine meadow in flower season.
4. **Food and waste.** Participants bring their own lunch. The plan does
   not say how waste is handled.

## Participant communication

A briefing email lists the kit list, the meeting point and the leaders'
phone numbers.
